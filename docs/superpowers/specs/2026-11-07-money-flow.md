The graph is a four-stage acyclic Sankey: income/funding, funding accounts, payment accounts, bills/unallocated. Account identities occur in both middle stages; same-account bands carry amounts not routed elsewhere. Internal transfer bands connect the appropriate account stages, permitting transfers in both directions without rendering cycles. This is an aggregate flow model, not paycheck-level tracing or chronological cash balance reconstruction.

For each account, direct passage = max(income - outgoing transfers, bills - incoming transfers, 0). Other funding supplies outgoing transfers/direct passage not covered by confirmed income. Other spending/retained funds receives residual amounts. Both account stages conserve flow; gap nodes are explicitly inferred residuals, not verified transactions or measured balances. Transfers use their outgoing month, including a receiving leg settled later. Hidden accounts participate in confirmed transfers, while income/bill selections inherit visibility rules. Duplicate onboarding can still cause duplicate bills/income and ambiguous transfers.

Flow data now includes all stored posted monetary activity in the selected month/currency without the 2,000-row UI cap, independently of global budgeting hide preferences. Pending/zero-value entries do not contribute. Every onboarded account appears in Settings, even without activity. Employer/confirmed-income naming and confirmed bill naming enrich labels but no longer gate inclusion. Unknown inflows are Other deposits/credits, and unconfirmed outflows are Spending. Interest/dividends remain Other income.

Confirmed deposit transfers and conservative unique debt-payment pairs are routed between accounts once, rather than duplicating their two legs as unrelated income/spending. Debt pairing requires opposite amount/currency, nearby dates, debt destination type, and payment wording on both sides. Matched movements appear when either leg posts in the month, with the possible adjacent-month counterpart disclosed. Card purchases remain spending; matched repayments are distinct debt movements, avoiding counting repayment as another purchase. Unmatched repayments/credits remain visible as spending/inflows, so incomplete matching can still inflate gross flows. Duplicate onboarding is not deduplicated. Security purchases/sales are not classified as savings or income automatically. Savings remains name-inferred modeled retained flow.
