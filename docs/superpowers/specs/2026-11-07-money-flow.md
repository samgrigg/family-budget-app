# Money Flow Sankey

Owner requested a new tab showing income-to-bill flows including internal transfers. Money Flow is a new navigation destination at /#money-flow. Uses actual recorded amounts for a selected calendar month and currency, not monthly estimates. Only confirmed income sources, saved bill transactions and confirmed transfer pairs are included. No new Plaid fetches/products.

The graph is a four-stage acyclic Sankey: income/funding, funding accounts, payment accounts, bills/unallocated. Account identities occur in both middle stages; same-account bands carry amounts not routed elsewhere. Internal transfer bands connect the appropriate account stages, permitting transfers in both directions without rendering cycles. This is an aggregate flow model, not paycheck-level tracing or chronological cash balance reconstruction.

For each account, direct passage = max(income - outgoing transfers, bills - incoming transfers, 0). Other funding supplies outgoing transfers/direct passage not covered by confirmed income. Other spending/retained funds receives residual amounts. Both account stages conserve flow; gap nodes are explicitly inferred residuals, not verified transactions or measured balances. Transfers use their outgoing month, including a receiving leg settled later. Hidden accounts participate in confirmed transfers, while income/bill selections inherit visibility rules. Duplicate onboarding can still cause duplicate bills/income and ambiguous transfers.

SVG band widths share one amount scale. Node labels abbreviate visually; titles and the full text flow list provide complete labels/amounts. List buttons expose selected connections in a status message. Chart scrolls horizontally on phones, while filters and text list wrap. Native keyboard controls, focus outlines, 44px controls, relative system text retained. SVG alone is not the accessible source of truth; all edges appear in text. Mobile chart panning is an intentional web adaptation. VoiceOver, large text, keyboard and actual-device verification remain outstanding. Filters support month input and currency choice; empty/loading/error states are rendered.
